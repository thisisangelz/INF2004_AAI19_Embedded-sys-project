# TCS34725 colour-mat calibration, 6 November 2026

First measurement of the robot's front colour sensor on the five-ring pink mat,
taken with `tcs34725_calib` (gain setting 1 = 4x, 103 ms integration). The
robot stood on its wheels so the sensor was at its mounted height. Each surface
was captured once: 20 readings at one spot, robot stationary.

## Findings

1. **`G/R` identifies the ring.** It falls by about 150 per ring, from 1013 on
   the white edge to 343 on ring 5, with no overlap between neighbours.
   Brightness `C` also falls steadily but depends more on height and lighting.
2. **The floor is darker than the white edge and ring 1**, with a `C` between
   rings 3 and 4. `C` alone would mistake the floor for a ring. Its `G/R` of 918
   is close to ring 1 (930), but `C` separates those two (3067 against 6274).
   Together, the two values separate the floor from every mat surface.
3. **The "other" surface matched ring 5** (`G/R` 344 against 343; `C` 1732
   against 1892, about 8% apart). Which object was measured was not recorded.
   If it was a shadow, chair leg or tape on the floor, a dark reading alone
   cannot be trusted as arrival. Repeat it with the object named.
4. The first version of the tool compared the floor as if it were lighter
   than the mat and reported `OVERLAP` for floor against white edge and ring 1.
   That check was wrong, not the data; the tool now compares the floor and the
   other surface in both directions.

## Proposed classification (to confirm with more spots)

Midpoints between neighbouring means:

| `G/R` x1000 | Surface |
|---|---|
| 972 and above | White edge |
| 856–971 | Ring 1 if `C` > 4670, otherwise floor |
| 707–855 | Ring 2 |
| 556–706 | Ring 3 |
| 412–555 | Ring 4 |
| 411 and below | Ring 5 |

Proposed arrival rule for the robot: **ring 4 or darker for three consecutive
readings, after a lighter mat ring has been seen**, plus a loose BLE check that
AP1 is nearby. Requiring the lighter rings first guards against a dark object
on the floor. Ring 4 keeps the robot's front clear of the beacon box.
